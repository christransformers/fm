## Suno.ai Style Prompting Specification & Formatting Rules
For maximum musical fidelity and distinctiveness in Suno v3.5 and v4, style prompts follow these strict structural standards:
- **Genre & Subgenre**: Precise subgenre anchors (e.g. `Tumblr dark indie-rock`, `outrun synthwave`, `math rock`, `modal spiritual jazz`).
- **Instrumentation**: Specific vintage/modern instruments & synth models (e.g. `chorus electric guitar`, `808 sub bass`, `Hammond B3 organ`, `Rhodes Mark I`, `Farfisa organ`).
- **Rhythm & Tempo**: Explicit BPM and feel descriptors (e.g. `115bpm driving 4/4`, `85bpm syncopated boom-bap`, `slow-burn 65bpm`).
- **Vocal Character**: Vocal delivery type, timbre, and gender (e.g. `nocturnal male baritone vocals`, `breathy Scandinavian female vocals`, `autotuned male rap`).
- **Production Atmosphere**: Spatial and acoustic treatments (e.g. `reverberant tape saturation`, `lo-fi vinyl noise`, `gated reverb snare`, `crisp hifi mix`).
